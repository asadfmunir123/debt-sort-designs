What could change

Small changes can make a big difference

Depending on your circumstances, there may be ways to make your debts feel more manageable.

You may be able to lower your monthly payments

A more manageable payment arrangement may be possible, depending on your circumstances.

You may be able to deal with interest and charges

Some solutions may reduce or freeze interest and charges, helping prevent debts from growing further.

You may be able to reduce the contact from creditors

If a suitable plan is put in place, creditor contact may reduce or stop.

You may be able to write off some unaffordable debt

In some circumstances, a portion of debt may be written off. This depends on your situation and any required creditor approval.

Please note

Solutions may not be suitable in all circumstances. Fees may apply. Your credit rating may be affected. We will explain the relevant risks, costs and alternatives before you decide.

How it works

A simple way to get started

Tell us what's happening

Complete our short form with a few details about your debts and circumstances. You do not need paperwork or exact figures to get started.

B

Talk it through with an adviser

We will get in touch for a confidential conversation, listen to what is happening, and explain the options that may be available to you.

Decide what feels right for you

We will explain the important details clearly, including any costs or possible effects on your credit file, so you can make an informed decision in your own time.

If an IVA is right for you, we will help you get it started. If it is not suitable, we can refer you to an alternative FCA-authorised organisation so you can explore other solutions.

My Debt Sort is a trading style of JT Maxwell Limited, who are a licensed insolvency practice. Andrew Ryder is authorised in the UK to act as an Insolvency Practitioner by the Insolvency Practitioners Association.

We specialise in Individual Voluntary Arrangements (IVA) in the personal insolvency sector. All advice is provided in reasonable contemplation of an IVA appointment. We will explain the options that may be available to you, including any fees, key risks and the possible effect on your credit file, before you decide whether to proceed. At any point should you no longer become eligible for an IVA, we will advise of this and refer you to an authorised third party.

All financial solutions are subject to eligibility and acceptance, depending on your chosen solution. Andrew Ryder is bound by the Insolvency Code of Ethics.

All IVAs have fees known as Nominee and Supervisor fees. There are also third-party costs payable, known as disbursements. The Nominee fee for assisting with the preparation of the statement of affairs and setting up the IVA is usually proposed at £2,000, and the Supervisor fee for handling the IVA is usually proposed at 15% of realisations. Our fees and costs are included within your payment and the timeframe of the IVA, all of which are agreed with your creditors at the outset.
